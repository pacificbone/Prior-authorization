# Hawaiʻi patient out-of-pocket estimator — public-source and calculation guide

**Reviewed November 8, 2026 (Hawaiʻi policy day). Synthetic-only trial.**

This workflow estimates *conditional cost sharing* for orthopedic procedures, HA/gel injections, and selected bone-health drugs in the Pacific Bone & Joint Prior Authorization app. The staff-facing **Patient costs** tab collects plan-neutral benefit assumptions and billable-component prices. It is **not** a real-time payer quote, eligibility check, contracted-fee determination, No Surprises Act Good Faith Estimate, advance explanation of benefits, coverage approval or financial counseling sign-off. **Do not enter protected health information, member identifiers, patient names, DOB, real clinical notes, or insurance portal credentials into this trial.** See [HIPAA release gates](HIPAA-RELEASE.md).

## What information is required to get a reliable estimate

1. **Exact insurance program, product and plan year** (not just the name of the carrier), network, billing benefit (medical, pharmacy, Part D), primary/secondary payer and applicable member eligibility.
2. **Procedure, codes and setting** (office, ambulatory surgery center, hospital outpatient or inpatient). The estimator itemizes by service type: surgery = surgeon, facility (outside the office), anesthesia (required except hand surgery), plus optional postoperative therapy and a separately billable implant; gel injection = administration plus the brand's J-code; office injections and pain procedures = professional fee, optional product, facility only outside the office, sedation optional; imaging and DXA = global/technical component plus an optional interpretation; therapy and DME = one supplier line; medications = one product claim plus optional administration and visit. Assistant surgeon, pathology, imaging ordered around a surgery and any other separately billed service are **not** itemized and must be added by hand. Use Medicare inpatient DRG/benefit period where appropriate rather than applying outpatient CPT percentages to the facility.
3. **Accurate allowed charges** matched to payer contract and plan, *actual billing entity and POS*, date, exact code/unit/modifier and facility. Hospital machine-readable gross charges and national network averages are **not** the patient's allowed amount. Facility MRFs often exclude independently billed surgeon/anesthesia. Prevent double-counting bundled implants/anesthesia.
4. **Current deductible remaining**, relevant fixed copay or coinsurance, plan's **remaining** individual medical out-of-pocket maximum, whether a service counts toward that maximum, and whether a secondary plan or financial assistance applies.
5. **Benefits and authorization confirmation**: coverage, prior approval, diagnosis/medical necessity, site of care, exceptions and whether the item is pharmacy- or medical-benefit supplied.

If mandatory component costs or their sharing rules are unknown, the estimator shows **no total**, along with missing inputs. Blank is not zero. If all required components have values, the total is labelled by confidence: **Assumption-only scenario** while coverage, network, secondary coverage, plan product, benefit channel or any required rate source is unverified; **Conditional modeled subtotal** only when every one of those is staff-verified; **Cash scenario** for self-pay. None of the three is a verified patient quote, and the exported worksheet prints the same CONFIDENCE line with the unverified items. The payer and plan line must be consistent (Original Medicare, self-pay, VA, TRICARE and workers' compensation each have exactly one line); a Medigap selection is estimated as Original Medicare with the supplement recorded as an unmodeled secondary. Reports are stamped fictional/synthetic. The app does not send information to any payer.

## Worked *fictional* examples — not Hawaii contracted rates

**Example 1, traditional Medicare Part B ASC joint surgery:** Suppose a *hypothetical* covered TKA had $2,000 surgeon allowed, $8,000 outpatient facility allowed, and $1,000 anesthesia allowed (not real Hawaii fee schedules). With a $283 remaining 2026 Part B deductible, no secondary coverage, and an assumed 20% beneficiary share on all three components, simple arithmetic produces **$2,426.40**. Actual OPPS/ASC cost sharing may have per-service statutory limits and different groupings. A patient-facing figure needs current allowed rates, real benefits and location-specific rules.

**Example 2, commercial in-network TKA:** Suppose $2,000 surgeon + $7,500 facility + $900 anesthesia are all correctly contracted allowed amounts (hypothetical), the remaining deductible is $1,500, post-deductible coinsurance is 20%, and the applicable medical OOP *remaining* is $700. The modeled share caps at **$700** for covered, qualifying in-network services. This is not a Kaiser/HMSA/UHA/Curative benefit default; the plan's actual copays and OOP aggregation may differ.

**Example 3, gel injection:** A valid model needs **both** the applicable 20610 or 20611 administration service and the exact hyaluronic acid brand, dose/units and J-code. The product might be pharmacy supplied or a medical-benefit buy-and-bill product. If the product or its allowed price is missing, **no total** is shown. Repeat series and preferred-product rules can change patient responsibility.

## Existing PBJ practice-intelligence source and future protected integration

The practice maintains a separate **private** intelligence workspace containing public machine-readable insurer negotiated **professional** rates and multiple hospitals' payer-specific **facility** rates. This public prior-auth repository does **not** publish, embed or duplicate private datasets, practice contracts or patient data.

A future protected connector must preserve:

- **Professional pricing:** actual code, year/source date, insurer plan, NPI, contracting TIN, billing class, office vs facility POS and modifiers. One NPI may have multiple rates; neither a median nor the minimum from a public TiC file is necessarily a true member contract.
- **Facility pricing:** exact hospital, CPT/HCPCS or inpatient **DRG**, setting, payer/plan, rate methodology (dollars vs percentage of charges or derived distribution), gross/cash/negotiated type and source version. An inpatient DRG cannot be compared as if it were a professional CPT fee. Hospitals may bundle implants and anesthesia in their facility rates.
- **Patient benefits:** separate authenticated eligibility and accumulator balances, covered/denied benefit status, referral/authorization and secondary insurance; not available in the current synthetic app.
- **Security:** covered production infrastructure, access controls, executed business associate agreements where required, encryption, least-privilege data access, audit log, bounded imports, validation/expiry and licensed payer APIs. No public Worker endpoint may expose private contract data or PHI.

Do not auto-populate the calculator from ambiguous price rows. If a rate is calculated as **percent of charges** without known applicable gross charge, or a record is a hospital inpatient bundle rather than the selected outpatient service, mark it **unknown**. A public negotiated rate is not a patient quote.

## Implementation status

- The **Patient costs** page calculates deductible allocation, coinsurance, copay, eligible remaining medical OOP, optional items and itemized scenario output based on **entered values only**.
- The calculator fails closed when mandatory rates/methods are absent, when Original Medicare inpatient Part A facility is treated as simple outpatient percentages, when the Original Medicare OPPS facility copayment has not been entered, when a pharmacy-benefit line is marked "deductible applies" (the medical deductible field is not a pharmacy deductible), when the payer and plan line are inconsistent, and when the service is marked noncovered. It does not cap cash-pay or pharmacy expenses against a *medical* OOP balance, and a line reduced to $0.00 by the remaining medical OOP limit is annotated "OOP limit reached".
- Medication lines are priced as a single claim (first dose); the worksheet states that per-dose claims and benefit-year crossings are not modeled.
- Payer cost tools are filtered by payer, plan line and benefit, so an HMSA commercial case no longer lists the Akamai Advantage EOC or the QUEST reference, and the state consumer FAQ is labelled as a general reference rather than the Med-QUEST handbook (the Med-QUEST site could not be re-verified from the build environment).
- The page includes procedures already supported in **Orthopedic Procedures** and selected bone-health drugs, including Evenity, Prolia, zoledronic acid and teriparatide.
- No live payer, MRF, member, EHR, claims, prior-auth submission, payment posting or Good Faith Estimate integration has been activated.
- The clinical practice must review calculations before relying on any amount.
